# PRIVACY POLICY

**App Name:** Social Media Usage Prediction App

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Social Media Usage Prediction App collects, uses, stores, and protects user information. The application uses Artificial Intelligence (AI), including LSTM and Transformer neural networks, to analyze smartphone application usage patterns and provide insights that support digital well-being.

We respect user privacy and aim to handle personal information transparently and responsibly.

## 2. Information We Collect

With the user's permission, the application may collect:

- **App Usage Data:** Application names, app-launch frequency, usage duration, session start and end times, and sequences of app usage.
- **Screen Time Information:** Daily and weekly screen time, frequently used applications, and usage patterns.
- **User Feedback:** Responses provided through features such as Quick Check and task-completion activities.
- **Device Information:** Basic technical information required for the application to function properly.
- **Camera Data (Optional):** If the Object Hunt feature is enabled, the camera may be used to detect objects and support task-based activities.

The app does not intentionally collect passwords, banking credentials, or private messages as part of its usage-prediction functionality. Notification content is not collected unless explicitly implemented and disclosed.

## 3. How We Use Information

The collected information may be used to:

- Analyze smartphone application usage patterns.
- Predict future app usage using LSTM and Transformer models.
- Display daily and weekly screen-time reports.
- Identify potentially excessive or unbalanced usage patterns.
- Provide personalized digital well-being recommendations.
- Support task-based activities and track user feedback.
- Improve application functionality and prediction performance.

The application is intended to promote awareness of digital habits and is not designed to make medical diagnoses.

## 4. Permissions and User Consent

The application may request access to Android Usage Access, camera functionality, and notifications where required by its features.

- Usage statistics are accessed only after the user grants the required permission.
- Camera access is requested only when a feature requires it.
- Users can revoke permissions through their device settings.
- Users should be informed about the purpose of each permission before granting it.

Revoking a permission may limit the functionality of the corresponding feature.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Usage information may be stored locally on the device or transmitted to a backend server for AI-based processing, depending on the application's implementation.

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Therefore, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Personal information is not sold to third parties.

Data may be processed by backend hosting providers or other technical services when necessary to operate the application. Such services should receive only the information needed for their intended purpose and be subject to appropriate privacy and security safeguards.

Information will not be shared for unrelated purposes without appropriate notice and, where required, user consent, unless disclosure is required by law.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information will be retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's features, maintain its operation, or meet applicable legal requirements.

Users should be provided with a way to delete stored usage information and associated feedback, where applicable. Users may also uninstall the application and revoke its permissions.

Uninstalling the app may not automatically delete information previously transmitted to a backend server. Any server-side data deletion process should be explained to users.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Where applicable, parental consent and additional safeguards will be implemented in accordance with relevant laws.

If information is found to have been collected unlawfully from a child, appropriate steps will be taken to address the issue.

## 9. User Rights

Subject to applicable law and the application's actual capabilities, users may request to:

- Understand what information is collected.
- Access or correct their personal information.
- Withdraw previously granted permissions.
- Request deletion of stored information.
- Raise questions or concerns about privacy practices.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version should be made available to users.

Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.

## 11. Disclaimer

This Privacy Policy is a project-specific draft intended to explain the application's expected privacy practices. It should be reviewed and updated to reflect the actual implementation, data flows, third-party services, and applicable legal requirements before publication or distribution.

Any consent required for data collection or processing must be obtained separately where applicable.
